# Sample Support Guide

This sample guide demonstrates how AI Support Copilot answers questions from uploaded files.

## Account access

Use your work email address to sign in to the support portal.

If you cannot sign in, first confirm that Caps Lock is off and that you are using the correct email.

Select **Forgot password** to receive a password-reset link.

The reset link expires after 30 minutes. Request a new link if it has expired.

If you no longer have access to your work email, contact the IT help desk.

Never share your password or verification codes with another person.

## Billing and Refunds

Your plan, renewal date, and invoices appear on the Billing page in account settings.

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact after each successful payment.

To update a payment method, open **Settings**, choose **Billing**, and select **Payment methods**.

If a payment fails, check that the card is active and that its billing address is current.

The system may retry a failed payment automatically. You can also retry it from the invoice page.

To ask for a refund, contact support within 14 days of the charge.

Include the invoice number and a short explanation; do not send full payment-card details.

Refund requests are reviewed within five business days.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

## Updating account details

Open **Settings** to change your display name or notification preferences.

Some account changes require you to verify your email address.

If a verification email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm the address is correct.

## Contacting support

Open the Help Center and select **Contact support** to create a request.

Choose the category that best matches your issue so it reaches the right team.

Describe what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Include the approximate time and any non-sensitive error message you saw.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or complete payment details.

## Service availability

Check the status page for current incidents and scheduled maintenance.

During an incident, save your work and try again after the status page reports recovery.

If the issue continues, contact support and reference the incident number.

## Troubleshooting

Refresh the page if a setting does not appear to update.

Sign out and back in if your account details look out of date.

Try a private browser window to check whether an extension is causing the issue.

If another browser works, update the original browser and try again.

Contact the support team if these steps do not resolve the problem.

Before contacting support, note the steps you already tried.

This helps the support team resolve your request faster.
